Our Mission

Not-so-fun fact: Over a whopping amount of NINETY PERCENT of children in the United States never get the chance to fulfill their childhood career dreams. From aspiring police officers to doctors and astronauts, only 5–10% succeed—largely because they lack consistent coaching, mentorship, and guidance along the way. Even worse — some children don't even know what they want to be when they grow up. And that's where we come in. We will build an AI and behavioral science platform that assesses each child's unique strengths, interests, and developmental needs, using this data to generate a personalized roadmap for success.
