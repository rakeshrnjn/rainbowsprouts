Who we are

You may be wondering, “What even is this project?” To answer your question, Rainbow Sprouts is a project for the youth competition Step Up Loudoun, created by brothers Aarmaan and Abhir Pahuja (8th and 6th graders from J. Michael Lunsford Middle School), along with their friends Aditya Ghosh and Aayan Priyal (8th and 6th graders from Willard Middle School).

We are really passionate about careers and figuring out what we want to be when we grow up, but we couldn’t find any career finders that felt reliable or actually useful. So, we decided to build our own, to help other students who are facing the same problem.

Again, as previously mentioned, we will create a test that determines possible careers for it's takers, and gives students advice on how to achieve that career by using resources that we reccomend. Just remember, if your qualities describe you with a job you like, it doesn't end there. You have to keep working hard to achieve that job. We will also assist you by connecting you with an ecosystem of trusted partners (including educators, coaches, content providers and experimental learning organizations)
